2. Minimum age

Thestia may be used from the age of 16. Someone aged 16 or 17 can also use the app without the consent of those with parental responsibility: under Sec. 3a JUG a person may consent themselves from their 16th birthday onwards, and nobody is prevented from using the app because consent is missing.

The parental approval is voluntary. If you are 16 or 17 and you value the supervision by your parents, you can request it: you then get a time window in which the app may be used, notices about new sparks and a protected area for your parents. The approval can be revoked at any time and without giving reasons. When it ends, the supervision ends as well, and the parents' email address is deleted.

Whether or not a parental approval exists: for 16 and 17 year olds the rules in section 4 always apply. From the 18th birthday onwards, the person decides themselves about the access of their parents.

  • You do not spread content that makes a person identifiable against their will, and you do not take or share screenshots or recordings of chats with other people without their consent.
  • You do not use automated tools to message users in bulk or to spread content automatically.

4. Minors and people who need particular protection

These additional rules apply to 16 and 17 year olds:

  • You only see other 16 and 17 year olds, and only they see you. Encounters are filtered on both sides: anyone aged 18 or older cannot reach you.
  • Your distance is not shown, not even roughly.
  • Your search radius is limited: at most 25 kilometres or people in the same federal state.
  • Transit Spark, the proximity feature for encounters on the move, is not available to you. It is for 18 year olds and older.
  • Your photos are only visible to other 16 and 17 year olds, and you cannot change that setting.
  • It is forbidden to offer or depict sex, nudity or sexual acts.
  • If a child or young person is a victim of sexual violence or exploitation, the reporting point at thestia.de/kinderschutz applies. We report confirmed cases to the competent bodies and suspend the account concerned immediately.
