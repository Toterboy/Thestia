Here you can manage the consent, set up notifications and limit the usage time.

Confirm consent

Your child has asked to use Thestia. For 16- and 17-year-olds the app asks for the consent of a legal guardian. That consent is voluntary: your child can use the app without it, just without supervision.

By confirming, you state that you are a legal guardian and that your child is allowed to use the app. You can revoke the consent at any time.

Notifications

When a spark appears between your child and another person, you get a short message. We do not give you any names and we do not send any profiles - only that something happened.

Usage time

You can define a time window in which the app may be used. Outside of it, the app shows a notice. The setting is protected by a four-digit PIN so that your child cannot change it on their own. The PIN is only checked on the server - the device does not know it.

The PIN is only needed when the usage time is limited. If you change it, the new one applies from now on.

Revoke consent

This ends the supervision immediately. Your child can keep using the app - they only lose the parental consent.
