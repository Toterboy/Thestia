Delete data

This is how you request the deletion of your data in Thestia – the dating app by Thoralf Illg –: either by category, without giving up your account, or completely including the account itself.

Thestia is a dating app for adults and for young people aged 16 and over. It is developed and operated by Thoralf Illg (provider according to the imprint: Thestia, the project by Thoralf Illg). This page belongs to Thestia and shows the ways in which you can request the deletion of your data.

Contact: support@thestia.de · Imprint: /en/impressum · Privacy policy: /en/datenschutz

Way 1: delete individual pieces of data in the app (account stays)

This is the quickest way, and it leaves your account untouched: you stay signed in, your email address stays, only the data you tick disappears.

  1. Open Thestia and go to Privacy & Account in the settings.
  2. Tap the entry Delete data.
  3. Tick what should disappear: profile photos, location data, profile details, likes, sparks, sent messages, devices and keys, mood and history, reports, Transit Spark data or the parental consent.
  4. Tap Delete and check the list in the confirmation dialog. The deletion is irreversible – there is no undo.

The page also offers Delete all data (keep account): this removes everything associated with your account, while the account itself remains.

Way 2: delete the whole account in the app

  1. Open Thestia and go to Privacy & Account in the settings, then to Privacy.
  2. Tap Delete account permanently.
  3. If two-factor authentication is enabled, an additional confirmation (TOTP code) is required.
  4. After the confirmation, the account and all its data are irreversibly deleted.

Way 3: in the browser, if you can no longer use the app

  1. Open the page /en/konto-loeschen. It describes which data is deleted and what proof is needed. You start the deletion itself in the app (Privacy & account → Delete account) or by email to support@thestia.de.
  2. Alternatively send an email to support@thestia.de with a request to delete your data. We confirm receipt and delete the data – usually within 30 days.

For way 3 we need proof that the request comes from the account itself (access to the registered email address, or the credentials chosen at registration). That is not a delay but the protection against deleting someone else's account.

The deletion in way 1 and way 2 is immediate: the data is removed from the server, not merely hidden.

If you delete only individual pieces of data, or nothing at all, the remaining data is kept only for as long as it is needed:

After the account has been deleted, only the technically necessary remains: the ban entries against bypassing child safety bans, the record of the parental consent and the email address in the hash directory, to prevent a new registration with the same address. These are deleted as soon as their purpose ends.

What we cannot delete

Received messages are the data of the other person – they disappear on their side, not on yours. And: data that another person has stored with us (for example a report about you) is deleted on their request, not on yours.
