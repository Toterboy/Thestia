Blind dates by personality, not photos.

With Thestia you get to hear what someone says before you see what they look like. Intros can be read and listened to, plus a small quiz about each other. Photos only appear once you have genuinely got to know each other. So the perfectly staged profile picture is not the centrepiece – the person behind it is.

FIVE WAYS TO SPARK A MATCH

• Find your Match: candidates without a photo, with an intro to read and listen to instead
• Quiz unlock: photos only after the quiz has been passed on both sides – enforced on the server
• Dating Hour: a weekly event with five-minute chats; a match only happens when both sides say yes
• Random chat: write anonymously to a random person
• Transit Spark: meet on the train – an encounter that can turn into a spark

PRIVACY IS NOT AN ADD-ON, IT IS THE ARCHITECTURE

• End-to-end encryption for every message, image and call (Signal protocol)
• Direct device-to-device connection via WebRTC – the server never sees the content
• Your location is never shared: others see at most „about X km away"
• No advertising or tracking SDKs, EU infrastructure, open source
• You decide whether your distance is shown at all – for minors it stays hidden

SAFETY FOR EVERYONE

• From the age of 18 in the Play Store: Google requires an age limit of 18 for dating apps. In other channels (F-Droid) the app can be used from the age of 16 with parental consent – minors stay invisible to adults
• Incoming chat images are blurred by default and only unlocked after confirmation
• Report and block, with every case reviewed by a real person
• Two-factor authentication and passkeys supported
• You can delete your account and your data yourself – right in the app

OPEN AND VERIFIABLE

Thestia is free software (AGPLv3). The complete source code is publicly readable, every decision in the project is documented, and the privacy policy, the threat model and the safety rules are all open. Built by a person with some help from artificial intelligence.
