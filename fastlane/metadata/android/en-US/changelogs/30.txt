Security and privacy in one update

Introductions that do not repeat
The idea cards for your introduction now rotate. Instead of tapping the
same four questions every time, you get a fresh mix each time you open
the app.

Important: confirm your e-mail address
A confirmed address is now required to receive sparks, give a thumbs up
and send messages. If yours is not confirmed, you will see a notice
where it matters. You can confirm it at any time in the settings.

Security number in chat
You can now make a chat wait until you have compared your security
numbers. That way nobody can swap the key of your chat and read along.

Nearby mode transmits irregularly
Your device no longer transmits on a fixed rhythm, but at irregular
intervals. It also only listens for Thestia signals now instead of every
Bluetooth message in the area.

Fixes
- Passkeys can be created more reliably
- Random chat and Dating Hour check again whether someone blocked you
- Location data is deleted automatically after 30 days
- Fewer profiles are shown when distance, age or pause do not allow it
