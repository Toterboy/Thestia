The keyboard now looks like the system keyboard - and you can switch

The app's own keyboard now looks like the one on your phone: light letter keys, darker function keys, a coloured enter key and a shadow under every key. With the button next to the input field you switch between the app's keyboard and your system's at any time - and the app keyboard still doesn't see a single letter of your messages.

Three bugs from testing fixed

In the interests area the text next to the "re-spark" button was broken up letter by letter. The preview of your own profile had no background, you could see the profile screen through it. And the keyboard setting did not apply everywhere.

And: after a meeting you can mute the app

Once you have met, the app offers the button "Thestia stays quiet until tomorrow": until the next morning there are no notifications. The app should celebrate being away instead of creating ties.
