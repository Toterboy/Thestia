Your profile saves again

In the last version, saving in the profile always failed - and nothing was saved at all: no name, no bio, no introduction. Two values were sent to the server wrongly. That is fixed.

The phone keyboard sees nothing

While you write, the app now uses its own keyboard. Your phone's keyboard is not even opened - it does not see a single letter and cannot learn anything from your text.

Going back in the random chat now asks

If you swipe back in the random chat, the app now asks whether the chat should be ended - just like the button in the top left corner. Before, you left without a question and the chat kept running.

Selected buttons stay where they are

When you select something in the profile settings, the button no longer jumps into the next line.

Your sparks no longer disappear

If the server could not be reached while loading the list of sparks, the app showed "no sparks". Now there is a note with "Try again", and your existing sparks stay where they are.

And: your own message is there right away, "Update" on the devices screen works again, and Transit Spark only shows the people who really match your description.

And more bugs fixed

Calling did not work at all before - the app crashed as soon as the call screen opened. After a call or a chat, the messages from your partner arrive again, the profile no longer loses your name, and you can leave the Dating Hour without the app crashing.

Dating that brings you back into real life

The app's slogan now greets you on the first screen. And the Dating Hour now pairs across genders: the event is for heterosexual people, so it consists of men and women. If you explicitly choose only your own gender in the profile, you are brought together with someone of that gender.

The terms of use are now in the app

You will find them under "Privacy & Account" - no detour through the browser.

Your age check now gets back to you

If your profile still has no check, you will find a note with the button "Verify now" on the home screen. And once your check is approved, you get a notification - even if the app is not open at that moment.

The chat stays usable even when the direct connection fails

If the direct connection between two phones does not come up, the chat keeps going - the messages arrive by a detour, and the screen stays usable. Before, a hanging connection setup could block the whole chat.

And: in the intro editor the text is now enough (a recording is optional), and the dialog for backing up your key stays open when the password does not fit yet.

No screen gets stuck any more

When the server does not answer, the app no longer waits forever: every server request is aborted after 20 seconds, and you get a message instead of a frozen screen.

And in the Dating Hour you now see when a message did not arrive

Until now your message could simply vanish without you noticing. Now the app tells you when it did not get out.

And: The age hint in the Dating Hour now really shows up

When you are ten years or more apart, the hint now actually appears in the chat - before, it was never shown.

And: The PIN dialog in the parental supervision no longer breaks the screen, and your data export only names the providers that really exist (no "Apple Inc." - the app is not in the App Store).
