Your profile saves again

In the last version, saving in the profile always failed - and nothing was saved at all: no name, no bio, no introduction. Two values were sent to the server wrongly. That is fixed.

The phone keyboard sees nothing

While you write, the app now uses its own keyboard. Your phone's keyboard is not even opened - it does not see a single letter and cannot learn anything from your text.

Going back in the random chat now asks

If you swipe back in the random chat, the app now asks whether the chat should be ended - just like the button in the top left corner. Before, you left without a question and the chat kept running.

Selected buttons stay where they are

When you select something in the profile settings, the button no longer jumps into the next line.

Your sparks no longer disappear

If the server could not be reached while loading the list of sparks, the app showed "no sparks". Now there is a note with "Try again", and your existing sparks stay where they are.

And: your own message is there right away, "Update" on the devices screen works again, and Transit Spark only shows the people who really match your description.
