Your name when you wave - you decide

The first time you open the Transit radar, the app asks you once:
should your name be visible when you wave to someone? There is no
default - you decide yourself. "No, I want to stay anonymous" is a
decision just as much as "Yes" is.

After that the question is gone, and you can change it at any time.
Your answer stays on the server - so your second device does not ask
again.

Important: this protects your name. Someone standing next to you can
still recognise you. The nearby list always stays anonymous.

Messages now arrive

If you got a notification before but the message itself stayed
invisible: that is fixed. The connection works, and the message now
arrives even if you had not opened the chat beforehand.

Tapping the notification takes you to the interests screen

Before, tapping only opened the app. Now you go straight to your
chats.

Unread chats are at the top

Chats with unread messages are now at the top and highlighted. Next to
the name there is a small number showing how many messages that person
has written.

In random chat you will not meet anyone you know

Anyone you already have a spark with is no longer found there - and
neither are blocked people.

Transit Spark hands out exactly one person

Transit Spark now hands out exactly one person. And if you already have
a spark with the person it found, the app tells you.
