No more duplicate notifications

One event, one notification. Some notifications used to arrive twice: a
spark in the radar triggered two messages, and a like added an extra
one even though the first had already said everything. Now every
action results in exactly one notification.

The "wink" reports itself again

When you wink at someone in the radar, that person gets a notification
again. It had gone missing for a while because a renamed key was being
looked for in the background.

Fixes

- The end-to-end chat works again: every transfer was previously
  discarded without notice
- A mutual like now creates a spark right away
- The chat background no longer disappears
- When you switch from Transit Spark to a chat, you are now asked
  whether the radar should keep running
- An "eye with a slash" now hides the like instead of rejecting it
