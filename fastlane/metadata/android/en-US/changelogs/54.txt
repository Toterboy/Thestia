The keyboard sees nothing

While you write, the app now uses its own keyboard. Your phone's
keyboard is not even opened - it does not see a single letter and cannot
learn anything from your text.

Pasting still works: there is a key for it, and a keyboard on a tablet
keeps working as before. The keyboard follows the language of the app
(German with umlauts, English with QWERTY).

And everywhere else in the app: the keyboard may not learn anything
from what you enter.

Transit Spark only shows the people who really match

The list of people nearby could be read out before there was a match at
all. Now only your own description counts - and the server checks it.
