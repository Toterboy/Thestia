Your number is right everywhere now

The small number in the tile counts that person's messages - and it is
the same on every device. Before, the app only counted what this device
had already seen.

Delete a message for everyone

Press and hold your own message to get "Delete for everyone". On the
other side it then says "This message was deleted". The server only
stores THAT it was deleted - never the content.

Transit Spark: everyone with your description

You now see everyone nearby whose features match yours - and you can wave
at several of them.

Transit Spark: say where you are

New on the radar: "Where are you right now?" - train, trade fair, event,
bar or somewhere else, plus your own words ("coach 3"). It is a
self-report - the app does not detect anything.

Transit Spark: your name, your decision

The first time you open the radar, the app asks whether your name may be
visible when you wave at someone. You decide that - and you can change it
at any time.

The nearby list no longer hides its size

And: A category without people now tells you which categories do have
people.
