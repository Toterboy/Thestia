More clarity for 16 and 17

Parental consent that is voluntary
If you are 16 or 17, you can add a parent. They receive an email with a
link, confirm it themselves and can undo it at any time. Nothing is
forced - and if no consent is given, the app is fully usable anyway.

Your decision at 18
From 18 onwards you decide yourself: end parental access, keep it for a
few more months, or keep it for now. The section stays visible in the
settings either way.

Notifications without interference
When a spark appears, a parent can get a short message - at the
interval they choose themselves. No names, no profiles, no message
contents. And a limited usage time, secured with a PIN that is only
ever checked on the server.

A tighter search radius
For 16 and 17, the radius is now at most 25 km - or only people from
your own federal state. From 18, everything stays freely selectable.

Fixes
- The distance between two profiles is no longer calculated wrongly
- Profiles with an old date of birth show the right results
