More security, more clarity

Verification that actually means something
Your video is now stored encrypted - nobody else can see it. And an
approval only counts if it was really granted: approval, rejection and
a running review are now told apart properly. Your verified age is what
counts, not what you entered.

Child safety that works when it matters
Concerns about child sexual abuse now have their own reporting path, and
it actually works. There is also a dedicated page with the contact
person and the reporting address: kinderschutz.thestia.de

Describable traits
In nearby mode you can now describe exactly what someone looks like -
from the shirt down to the shoes and hair colour. The traits are sorted
by category, and you get a badge once your description is complete.

A bigger privacy promise
No TURN server any more, no location data that is kept longer than
needed, and a visible notice when someone sees your distance. You can
delete your data yourself at any time - without asking support.

Access only for verified accounts
Without verification you no longer see any suggestions. That protects
against age entries that are not true.

Fixes
- Reporting works again - including for inappropriate images
- Password reset and resending the confirmation mail work
- Profiles are no longer shown with the wrong age
- Favourite song, favourite band and birthday style are kept
- Several places where text was cut off
