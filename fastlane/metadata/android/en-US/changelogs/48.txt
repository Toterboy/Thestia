Safer connections

The app now checks its encrypted connections more strictly than
before. On top of that it ships a more current WebRTC, so the
connections between two devices are on a secure footing as well.

A more stable chat

Messages arrive more reliably, the chat background no longer
disappears, and a "wink" in the radar reports itself again.

Fixes

- The end-to-end chat works again: every transfer was previously
  discarded without notice
- A mutual like now creates a spark right away
- The chat background no longer disappears
- When you switch from Transit Spark to a chat, you are now asked
  whether the radar should keep running
- An "eye with a slash" now hides the like instead of rejecting it
