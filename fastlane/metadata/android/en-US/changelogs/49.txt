Meeting people by their interests

"Find your Spark" now has two ways: the random introduction as before -
or you browse through your interests. You see who is into "music",
"sports" or "gaming", read their introduction and can like them. The
age limit applies just as strictly as everywhere else: 16- and
17-year-olds only ever see 16- and 17-year-olds.

Your introduction works as text

Until now the recording was mandatory. Your text is enough now - the
recording is optional. If you have one, it stays encrypted and is only
heard once there is a spark.

Images in the chat are visible again

Images you sent in the chat were only shown as a placeholder. Now the
image is visible - and it stays pixelated until you release it
yourself.

Your introduction has a time display

While listening to an introduction you can now see how long it is and
how far you are - with a progress bar and the time.

Your profile photos stay

After saving, the photos in tiles two and three disappeared again.
That is fixed.

Small things in the menu

The three-dot menu is now tidier: every group has a heading, and a
swipe closes the screen without scrolling on in the background.
