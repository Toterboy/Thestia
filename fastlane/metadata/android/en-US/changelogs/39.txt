Your location belongs to you

Your city is no longer collected. Only coordinates on a 5 km grid and
your federal state are stored - and the grid is enforced server-side.
The distance display is off and becomes your decision: switched on,
others see one step of 10 km, below 5 km nothing at all.

Delete data without giving up your account

Photos, location, profile, likes, sparks, your messages, device list,
mood, reports, transit data and the parental consent - individually or
all of it. Your login stays.

More clarity for 16 and 17

Parental consent that is voluntary: you can add a parent, they confirm
it themselves and can undo it at any time. If no consent is given, the
app is fully usable anyway. From 18 onwards you decide yourself. And
for 16 and 17 the radius is now at most 25 km - or only people from
your own federal state.

Fixes

- The end-to-end chat works again: every transfer was previously
  discarded without notice
- The parental section no longer loads forever
- Transit Spark no longer counts other Bluetooth devices and finds
  devices again on Android 11
- The heading in the feature sheet is no longer in the status bar
